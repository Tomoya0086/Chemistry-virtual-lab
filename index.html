Curriculum Foundations

Introduction to Cheminformatics

Cheminformatics combines chemistry, computer science, and information technology to represent, simulate, and analyze molecular structures and their physicochemical behaviors computationally.

SMILES Representation

SMILES (Simplified Molecular Input Line Entry System) encodes molecular connectivity into a concise ASCII text string.

  • Atoms: Elements indicated by standard symbols (C, N, O, P, S). Lowercase represents aromaticity (c, n, o).
  • Bonds: Single bonds implicit, = for double bonds, # for triple bonds.
  • Branches: Parentheses (...) specify substituent branching.
  • Rings: Digits 1, 2, ... designate ring-closure bond points.

3D Molecular Geometries

Computational methods predict spatial coordinates from 2D connectivity graphs using energy minimization algorithms:

  • Ball and Stick: Atoms as spheres and covalent bonds as rigid rods to emphasize stereochemistry.
  • Stick Model: Covalent bond network highlighting hybridization and bond angles.
  • Space Filling (CPK): Van der Waals atomic spheres demonstrating overall steric volume.
  • Wireframe: High-speed skeletal bond topology for large macromolecular conformations.
