Module 03

Effective Nuclear Charge (Zeff)

An outer electron never feels the full nuclear pull — inner electrons screen it. Slater's rules estimate that screening constant S, so that Zeff = Z − S. Choose an element and an orbital to see every step.

Slater's rules in brief

Electron's positionContribution to S
Same group (ns, np), each other e⁻0.35
Same 1s group, the other e⁻0.30
Shell n−1 (when electron is in s or p)0.85
Shell n−2 or deeper1.00
Any inner e⁻ (when electron is in d or f)1.00
Electrons in higher groups0.00
